# Source provenance and attribution

The experiment implementations were extracted from the `model-forensics`
research workspace in `gutenbergpbc/code`. `manifests/source-code.json` records
the original relative path and pre-migration hash of each copied source file.
The migration changes import roots, local artifact locations, entrypoints and
documentation; new runs receive new scientific source identities.

The Sales environment derives from the specification-gaming evaluation code.
Its original MIT copyright/permission notice is preserved in `LICENSE`:
Copyright (c) 2025 Kei Nishimura-Gasparian. This notice does not establish a
license for every separately sourced dataset or task fixture.

TerminalWrench task snapshots retain their original instructions, environment,
tests and metadata. Trace provenance and task identifiers remain in the data
archives. Preserve upstream attribution and check distribution terms when
publishing third-party task data. The generated emotion corpora retain their
writer and review provenance; they are model-reviewed development fixtures,
not human-certified emotion labels.

The copied Inter font includes its original license under the corresponding
`_kit/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt`. Model checkpoints are downloaded separately under
their own terms and are never redistributed in this repository.
